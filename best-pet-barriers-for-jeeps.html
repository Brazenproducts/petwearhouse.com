Choosing the Right Pet Barrier for Your Vehicle

Vehicle-Specific vs. Universal

If you drive a Jeep Wrangler, Gladiator, or Ford Bronco, go vehicle-specific. The Bartact Spare Tire Trash Bag is designed specifically for these vehicles and fits like it was factory-installed. Universal barriers work in a pinch, but they never fit as cleanly and often require awkward mounting solutions.

Soft vs. Rigid Barriers

Rigid barriers (metal or hard plastic) are more secure but harder to install and remove. Soft barriers like the Bartact options are easier to set up, store when not in use, and often serve multiple purposes. For most pet owners, a quality soft barrier is the better choice — especially if you frequently switch between dog-hauling and cargo-hauling configurations.

Multi-Function Wins

This is where Bartact really shines. Every one of their pet barriers doubles (or triples) as something else — storage, organization, sun shade. In a Jeep where space is already limited, gear that serves multiple purposes isn't just nice to have, it's essential. Why install a barrier that only does one thing when you can get one that also organizes your gear?

The Jeep Owner's Bottom Line

If you're a Jeep or Bronco owner with a dog, the Bartact lineup is purpose-built for your exact situation. The Spare Tire Trash Bag & Pet Divider is our top pick for its ingenious design and perfect fit. For any vehicle, the Between Front Seat Storage Bag is a universal solution that works brilliantly. These aren't generic pet products — they're engineered by people who understand off-road vehicles and the people (and dogs) who drive them.
