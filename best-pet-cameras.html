You leave for work. Your dog gives you the look — the one that says "you're abandoning me and I will never recover from this." You spend the next eight hours wondering if they're okay, if they're destroying the couch, or if they're just sleeping in a sunbeam like the drama queen they are.

Pet cameras let you check in. The good ones let you talk to your dog, toss them a treat, and get alerts when they're barking or moving around. Some even use AI to detect specific behaviors. It's not a replacement for being home, but it's the next best thing.

We tested and researched every major pet camera on the market. Here are the five that actually deliver a good experience.

How to Choose a Pet Camera

Treat Tosser: Fun or Gimmick?

Honestly? It's both — and that's fine. Treat tossers are genuinely useful for positive reinforcement when you're away, and dogs love the interaction. But if you're on a budget, a regular camera with two-way audio does 90% of what a treat camera does. The audio alone is enough to comfort most dogs (or tell them to get off the counter).

Subscription Costs

This is the hidden cost of pet cameras. Many cameras work fine without a subscription for live viewing, but recording, smart alerts, and AI features usually require a monthly plan ($3-$15/month). eufy is the notable exception — local storage, no subscription. Factor this into your decision.

Night Vision Quality

Your dog doesn't stop being interesting when the lights go off. Good night vision (especially color night vision) lets you check on your dog at any hour. The Wyze Cam v3's Starlight sensor is particularly impressive for the price — it produces usable color images in very low light.

Two-Way Audio

Every camera on this list has two-way audio, and for good reason — it's the most useful pet camera feature. Being able to talk to your dog (and hear them) provides real comfort for both of you. Some dogs respond to voice commands through the speaker, which can be helpful for redirecting unwanted behavior.

Our Take

The Furbo 360° is the best dedicated pet camera — the treat tosser, auto-tracking, and bark alerts make it purpose-built for dog owners. If budget is a concern, the Wyze Cam v3 is an absurd value at under $30. And if you hate subscriptions, the eufy S350 gives you premium features with local storage and no monthly fees.
