Best Pet Cameras in 2026

Updated April 2026

Pet cameras split into two groups: cameras that are actually useful, and cameras that are basically app-connected toys. The best ones give you stable video, a decent app, and enough reliability that you do not want to throw them through a wall by day three.

Top Pick

Furbo 360 Dog Camera

Furbo is still the obvious pick for people who specifically want a pet-first camera experience. The treat toss gimmick only matters if the camera and app are good enough to support it, and Furbo is one of the few that usually does.
