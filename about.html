Who We Are

Pet Wearhouse is an independent pet gear review site. We're part of the What Are Best review network, which covers honest product reviews across dozens of categories — from seat covers to cordless tools to pet gear.

We started Pet Wearhouse because we were tired of pet gear "reviews" that are really just affiliate link farms with no actual testing or analysis. Every product on our site has been evaluated based on verified customer reviews, return rate data, material quality, and real-world performance.

How We Rank Products

Our ranking methodology is straightforward and transparent:

  • Verified reviews: We analyze thousands of verified Amazon reviews, looking for patterns in praise and complaints. A product with 10,000+ reviews at 4.5+ stars tells a more reliable story than any single reviewer.
  • Return rate data: High return rates signal problems that reviews alone don't capture. We factor this into every ranking.
  • Material and build quality: We evaluate construction materials, hardware quality, stitching, and durability indicators.
  • Price-to-quality ratio: The best product isn't always the most expensive. We look for the sweet spot where quality meets value.

What We Don't Do

  • We don't accept free products in exchange for reviews
  • We don't accept sponsored placements or paid rankings
  • We don't have manufacturer relationships that influence our picks
  • We don't recommend products we wouldn't buy for our own dogs

Affiliate Disclosure

Pet Wearhouse is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.
