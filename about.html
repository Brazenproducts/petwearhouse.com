About Pet Wearhouse

Independent review site

Pet Wearhouse exists for one reason: most pet gear coverage is either generic affiliate fluff or cute branding with no real opinion behind it. We prefer useful over cute, durable over trendy, and product picks that make sense in the real world.

We cover leashes, harnesses, pet travel products, pet barriers, dog beds, trackers, and vehicle-friendly gear. Where BarkTact or Bartact make the best product for the job, we say that directly. Where an Amazon product is the better fit for most buyers, we say that too.
