Best Dog Car Seat Covers of 2026

Protect your seats from fur, scratches, mud, and drool

Home › Best Dog Car Seat Covers

Dog hair embeds in fabric seats. Muddy paws stain leather. Nails scratch everything. A good car seat cover pays for itself the first time your dog jumps in after a rainy hike. We tested covers for waterproofing, durability, fit, and ease of cleaning — because a cover that's a pain to wash doesn't get washed.
